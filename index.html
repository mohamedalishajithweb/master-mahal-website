About Master Mahal

Master Mahal is a spacious and elegant wedding venue located in Eruvadi, designed to host weddings, receptions, engagement ceremonies, and family celebrations with comfort and style.

Featuring a grand hall, dining facilities, lift access, ample parking, and accommodation for large gatherings, Master Mahal provides the ideal setting for memorable occasions.
